3. Written absence marks
------------------------
On sheet 2 the absence of student 10009306 was recorded by writing a struck-out
"-ab-" mark into the signature cell rather than by leaving it empty. The cell
therefore contains as much ink as a signature:

    colour ratio 0.1127, dark-ink ratio 0.0957  ->  9.39x the threshold

An ink-ratio rule alone would call that cell present. It is classified absent by
the strike-through rule instead: a long thin stroke sitting beside the written
token at the same height, which is what distinguishes a struck-out mark from an
underlined signature.

    Detected written absence marks : 1 of 1
    False positives on the other 29 cells : 0

4. Decision margin

    Strongest blank cell : 0.65x the threshold

6. Class signature audit sheet
------------------------------
Command: python audit_sheet.py

One grid covering the whole class: a row per student, a column per lecture, and
in each cell the extracted signature with a verdict. Each signature is compared
with the same student's other signatures, the median score is kept, and that
median is read against the genuine and impostor distributions to give the
posterior probability that the sample resembles a different writer.

    Cells judged                             : 25 signed, 5 absent
    Flagged for review (borderline or above) : 12 of 30
    Students with every sample flagged       : 2

The last number matters most. When every sample of one student is flagged, nothing
can be said about which signature is the odd one, because the reference set
itself is inconsistent. On this dataset that happens for 10009304 and 10009306,
whose signatures overrun the cell and are clipped differently on each sheet, so
the sheet labels those rows "check the capture, not the student".

Important limitations
---------------------
The 100% baseline result applies to the five supplied signing sheets only. It is
not a claim of general accuracy on unseen layouts, classes of a different size
or other cameras.

The written-absence-mark rule is calibrated on a single example, because the
dataset contains exactly one such mark. It is correct on the supplied sheets and
never fired on a genuine signature, but its recall falls under degradation and
it should not be relied on until more samples are available.

An ROC area of 0.82 means the signature measure is only weakly discriminative.
It is reported as a review prompt and must never be treated as proof of identity
or of forgery.
